Binary (strict):
- Likely unsuitable - areas with probabilities predicted below the presence threshold.
- Possibly suitable - areas with probabilities above the presence threshold, but with confidence values below 50 and probabilities below the mid-point of the minimum and maximum predicted presence probabilities.
- Likely suitable - areas with probabilities above the presence threshold, but with either confidence values below 50 OR probabilities below the mid-point of the minimum and maximum predicted presence probabilities.
- Very likely suitable - areas with probabilities above the presence threshold and with both confidence values above 50 and probabilities above the mid-point of the minimum and maximum predicted presence probabilities.

Binary:
- Unsuitable - areas with probabilities below the presence threshold.
- Suitable - areas with probabilities above the presence threshold.

Binary (envelope): This map shows the binary distributions where the distribution for species dependent on hard substrate has been masked by a layer corresponding to areas with >= 10% hard bottom.

Binary (confidence):
- Unsuitable (high confidence) - areas with probabilities below the presence threshold and with confidence scores above 50.
- Unsuitable (low confidence) - areas with probabilities below the presence threshold but with confidence scores below 50.
- Suitable (low confidence) - areas with probabilities above the presence threshold but with confidence scores below 50.
- Suitable (high confidence) - areas with probabilities above the presence threshold and with confidence scores above 50.

Delta: The scale represents relative probabilities above the presence threshold. Areas with a value of zero represent areas below the presence threshold. Areas with a value of 1 represent the lowest probability above the presence threshold. Areas with a value of 100 represent the highest probability above the presence threshold.

Probability: The scale represents predicted probability outputs from the ensemble species distribution model.

Confidence: The scale represents model confidence measured as agreement between members of the ensemble species distribution model (committee averaging). A value of 100 indicates that all members predicted the same outcome (presence or absence). A value of 0 indicates maximum disagreement between the members (half of the members predicting presence and half predicting absence).
